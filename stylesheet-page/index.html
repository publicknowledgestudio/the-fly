Palette
Browser effects

The three palettes are recolours of one painting, and they line up exactly: 0 px drift, edge correlation 0.93 (P2) and 0.95 (P3). That means Page 2 can crossfade toward pink a little more each time the fly lands, instead of cutting. The two effect buttons preview effects the browser will add over every layer. Grain + dither brings the texture closer to refs 1 and 2 and gives separately generated layers the same surface. Paint boil is the frame-to-frame wobble of the rotoscoped references.

Source

What we took from the references

Medium
Gouache and oil pastel painted over footage; refs 3 and 4 are frame-by-frame sequences.
Colour
Colours are assigned, not realistic: lilac or plum skin, 4–6 flat saturated colours per frame, in contrasting pairs.
Edges
No outlines. Rough, dry-brush edges on every shape.
Texture
Scumbled strokes and paper grain; dither and halftone in refs 1 and 2 (added in the browser).
Light
Hard sun, shown only as flat cast-shadow shapes. No gradients.
Camera
Photographic: wide lens, top-down views, abrupt crops. This will shape Setups C and D.
Figures
Almost faceless; posture does the acting.

Before layering

Open calls

  1. Should the beanie stay green on every page?The P2 recolour turned it teal and P3 turned it plum. Recommendation: keep it green on all three pages so the person stays recognisable while everything around them changes.
  2. How strong should the grain + dither be?The preview above is a starting value. It's one setting in the browser and can be adjusted later without regenerating anything.
  3. Fly design: plum body, red eyes, teal legs.Recraft invented these colours, and they fit all three palettes. Approve, or say what to change.
